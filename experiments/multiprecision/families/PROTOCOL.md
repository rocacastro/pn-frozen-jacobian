# Family timing protocol

The family comparison is fixed before timing. `P_N` and Shamanskii are selected independently by the same algebraic reference-index convention (`kappa=1`), giving `P3/S3` on `H1` and `P9/S10` on `H5`.

Every group uses the same starting distance, working precision, verification precision, warmup count, repetition count, stopping rule, and paired execution structure. The precision-control runs are outside the timed comparison. The reference work model is not calibrated to multiprecision elapsed time.

The archived timing results are implementation- and platform-specific. They do not establish a universal timing ordering or isolate a causal effect attributable only to memory.
